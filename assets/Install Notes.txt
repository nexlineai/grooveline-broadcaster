GROOVELINE BROADCASTER - INSTALL NOTES (macOS)
==============================================

1. Drag "Grooveline Broadcaster" onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open it from Applications. macOS asks to allow the camera and the
   microphone: click OK. The app needs both to show your input and to stream.
3. Sign in, and the app loads your slot by itself.

If macOS says it can't check the app for malicious software (only on builds
that are not notarized yet):
  - macOS 15 Sequoia and newer: try to open it once, then go to
    System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".
  - macOS 14 and older: right-click the app > Open > Open.
  You only need to do this once.

Apple Silicon needs macOS 12 or newer. Intel Macs need macOS 10.15 or newer.

Help: https://grooveline.org/start-dj#install-mac   |   support@grooveline.org

Getting on air
  1. Sign in with your grooveline.org account. No account? Sign up and book a slot on grooveline.org.
  2. Check your slot on ON AIR: it loads by itself.
  3. Pick your input and play music. The meters on the right must move.
  4. Run a Stream check. When our server hears you, press Listen to hear what we receive.
  5. When your slot starts, press GO LIVE. Press STOP when you're done.
Setup tips

Mixer or controller with USB audio: pick its input (for example "Traktor Kontrol Z2" or "DJM-450"). If it has 4 or more inputs, pick the pair your master or record output is on.

DJ software only (Mixxx, Traktor in Wine…): pick "Monitor of …" for the output your DJ software plays to.

PipeWire and PulseAudio both work. Linux uses the system ffmpeg: install it with your package manager if the app says it's missing.

Mixer or controller with USB audio: pick its input (on a Traktor Kontrol Z2: "Master Input"). If your DJ software holds the device through ASIO, this app may not get it: use the mixer's own record output or a virtual cable.

DJ software only: install a virtual cable (VB-Cable), set your DJ software's master output to "CABLE Input", and pick "CABLE Output" here.

Mixer or controller with USB audio: pick its input. Allow microphone access when macOS asks (System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone).

DJ software only (Rekordbox, Serato, djay): install BlackHole 2ch, set your DJ software's master output to BlackHole, and pick "BlackHole 2ch" here. To hear yourself too, use a Multi-Output Device in Audio MIDI Setup.

How to fix
The input stopped or is unavailable
  • Replug the mixer or interface, press ↻ next to the input and pick it again.
  • Close other apps that may hold it exclusively (DJ software set to ASIO, another streaming app).
  • No meter movement while music plays: wrong input or wrong channels. Try the other pairs.
Camera
  • Check it's plugged in and not open in another app (OBS, a browser tab, a video call).
  • Without a camera, a video slot streams your audio to the radio, so you're still on air.
The server refused the password (401)
  • A slot booked minutes ago, or your first stream check, needs about a minute to be ready. The app keeps trying for 2 minutes.
  • Stream details expire when your slot ends. Sign out and in again to load your current slot.
The server is still holding your previous connection (403)
  • After a dropped connection, the server keeps your old stream open for a while. It lets go by itself (about a minute). Keep the app open: it retries every 15 s and you're back on air on its own.
Connection dropped or nothing is leaving your computer
  • The app reconnects by itself until you press STOP.
  • Check your internet. For a live set a network cable is safer than Wi-Fi.
  • If it keeps dropping, save the log (LOG › Save log…) and send it to us.
The encoder failed
  • This shouldn't happen. Save the log (LOG › Save log…) and send it to info@grooveline.org with your system (About › Copy).
